School Newsletter

Stay updated with the latest news, events, and announcements from NNSS Calabar

Enrollment Open for 2026/2027 Academic Session

May 11, 2026

Admissions are now open for the 2026/2027 academic session for JSS1 and SS1. Interested parents and guardians should visit the school administration office or contact us for the admission forms and requirements. The new session begins in September 2026.

SS3 Students Achieve 73% WAEC Pass Rate

April 28, 2026

We are thrilled to announce that our graduating class has achieved an outstanding 73% pass rate in the 2026 WAEC examinations. This remarkable result is a testament to the dedication of our teachers and the hard work of our students. Congratulations to all our SS3 graduates!

New Digital Portal Launched

April 15, 2026

NNSS Calabar has launched a comprehensive digital portal system for students, teachers, and administrators. The portal allows students to view their results online, teachers to upload scores, and administrators to manage school data efficiently. All users should log in with their credentials to access the new system.

Inter-House Sports Competition 2026

March 20, 2026

The annual inter-house sports competition was a huge success with students from all houses (ADEKEYE, ADENIYI, AJADI and BAJE) participating enthusiastically. House ADEKEYE emerged as the overall champions. Thank you to all teachers, parents, and students who made this event possible.

Library Expansion Complete

March 5, 2026

The school library expansion project has been completed successfully. The new wing features additional reading areas, a digital resource center with computers, and an expanded collection of over 5,000 new books. Students are encouraged to utilize this valuable resource for their studies.

Parent-Teacher Association Meeting

February 15, 2026

The PTA meeting held on February 15, 2026, discussed important matters including the new academic calendar, school improvement projects, and the digital portal initiative. All parents and guardians are encouraged to attend future meetings to stay involved in their children's education.

Naval Career Day 2026

January 20, 2026

Naval Career Day was organized to expose students to various career paths within the Nigerian Navy. Naval officers visited the school to give presentations on life in the Navy, educational requirements, and opportunities available. Students were inspired by the stories of naval heroes and the opportunities that await them.

Second Term Examination Results Released

January 10, 2026

Second term examination results for the 2025/2026 academic session have been released. Students can now log in to the school portal to view their individual results and subject scores. Parents are encouraged to review their children's performance and discuss areas for improvement with subject teachers.
