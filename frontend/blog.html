School Blog & History

Explore the rich history, achievements, and stories of NNSS Calabar

May 29, 2025

STEM Competition won by NNSS CAL [2025]

The Chief of Naval Staff organizes yearly competitions for all NN welfare schools. Through determination, hardwork and commitment, NNSS Calabar emerged first at the command level and third nationwide.

February 20, 2026

Engineering Department

NNSS Calabar in its bid to produce outstanding individuals in works of life, provides a platform to give engineering a practical means of learning through facilitated labs and termly external competitions with other schools.

December 7, 2025

Students' Carol night 2025

NNSS Calabar pays keen attention to the religious growth and moral development of its students. It organizes a yearly carol night involving candlelight processions, inter-house carol singing competitions, a fireworks display and much more.

April 5, 2026

Night View of the School Environment

At NNSS Calabar, the management treats student security as a top priority. Its newly installed NDDC streetlights aid in the lighting of the premises, making the environment much safer.

April 25, 2026

Sport and Activities

The school provides facilities and equipment for sports activities, improving students' athleticism and sportsmanship. It also hosts sessional cultural days to celebrate our cultural heritage.

May 8, 2026

Academic Excellence

NNSS Calabar has consistently produced students with outstanding grades — from JAMB to WAEC to NECO, and even the JSS3 BECE.
